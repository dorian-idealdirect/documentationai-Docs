## Quick Steps

To create your first project in Idealsync:

1. Go to your Idealsync dashboard.
2. Click the `+ New Project` button at the top right.
3. Enter a name and optional description.
4. Select a workspace (if prompted).
5. Click `Create`.

Your new project will now appear on the dashboard. Click it to start adding and organizing your documentation immediately.
