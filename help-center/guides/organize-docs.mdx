## Get Started

Structure keeps information clear for your team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add sections">
    From your project page, click `Add Section` to group related pages (e.g. Onboarding, API, Guides).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create new docs">
    Within a section, click `New Page` to start a document—use the editor to add text, images, or code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder easily">
    Drag and drop sections or pages in the sidebar to structure your docs as needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
