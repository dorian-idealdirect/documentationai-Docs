## Get Started in Minutes

Welcome to Idealsync. Follow these steps to create your account, set up your first workspace, invite your team, and organize your project documentation. You'll be up and running with less busywork and more momentum in under 10 minutes.

<Callout kind="info">

**Prerequisites:**
- A work email address
- Team member emails for invitations (optional)

</Callout>

## Step-by-Step Setup

Use the guided process below to launch your documentation space.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account" icon="user-plus">
    Visit [https://app.idealsync.com/signup](https://app.idealsync.com/signup).

    Enter your work email and create a secure password. Verify your email via the confirmation link sent to your inbox.

    <Image
      src="https://via.placeholder.com/800x400/ef4444/ffffff?text=Sign+Up+Screen"
      alt="Idealsync sign up screen"
      width="800"
      height="400"
    />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Your Workspace" icon="home">
    After signing in, click **Create Workspace**.

    Name it (e.g., `Project Alpha Docs`) and add a description like "Central hub for all project documentation".

    Customize your brand color to `#ad351f` and add your slogan: "Less busywork. More momentum."
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite Team Members" icon="users">
    In the workspace settings, go to **Team** > **Invite Members**.

    Enter team emails separated by commas. Set roles: `Admin`, `Editor`, or `Viewer`.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Bulk Invite" icon="upload">
        Paste a list of emails:

````text
team@example.com, dev@idealsync.com, manager@company.com
````

      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Single Invite" icon="user">
        Add one email at a time with a custom message.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Organize Your First Project Docs" icon="book-open">
    Create your first document: **New Doc** > Name it `Project Overview`.

    Use Markdown for structure:

    <CodeGroup tabs="Markdown,Preview">
```markdown
# Project Alpha

## Goals
- Deliver MVP by Q2
- Reduce busywork by 50%

## Next Steps
1. Review requirements
2. Assign tasks
```

```html
<h1>Project Alpha</h1>

<h2>Goals</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Deliver MVP by Q2</li>
  <li>Reduce busywork by 50%</li>
</ul>

<h2>Next Steps</h2>
<ol>
  <li>Review requirements</li>
  <li>Assign tasks</li>
</ol>
```
    </CodeGroup>

    Share the doc link with your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>
